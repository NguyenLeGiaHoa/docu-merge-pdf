# Privacy Policy for DocuMergePDF

**Last updated:** November 5, 2026

## 1. Overview
DocuMergePDF ("we", "our", or "the Application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices for DocuMergePDF.

## 2. 100% Offline & Local Processing
DocuMergePDF is built from the ground up as a fully private, offline desktop utility:
- **No Cloud Uploads:** All document mail merges, spreadsheet parsing, and PDF generation occur strictly in memory and on your local workstation.
- **Zero Data Collection:** We do not collect, view, transmit, or monetize any personal information, customer lists, emails, templates, or generated PDFs.
- **No Telemetry or Tracking:** The Application contains no analytical trackers, behavioral monitoring, or advertising frameworks.

## 3. System Permissions
DocuMergePDF requires file system permissions strictly to perform the operations you request:
- **Read Access:** To read Word templates (.docx) and Excel/CSV data files (.xlsx, .csv) that you select.
- **Write Access:** To output individual merged PDF files into your chosen destination folder.
- No other directories or personal files are accessed.

## 4. Third-Party Services
DocuMergePDF operates completely standalone without connections to third-party cloud servers or third-party APIs.
