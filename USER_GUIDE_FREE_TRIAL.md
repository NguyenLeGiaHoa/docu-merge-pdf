## 🛠️ 3. STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW

### Step 1: Prepare Your Word Template
In Microsoft Word, simply type double curly brackets around any text you want to personalize:
> "This certifies that **{{FullName}}** has completed **{{CourseName}}** on **{{Date}}**."

### Step 2: Drop Your Files into DocuMergePDF
* Drag your Word template (`.docx`) into the **Template Dropzone**.
* Drag your Excel roster (`.xlsx`, `.csv`) into the **Data Dropzone**.
* DocuMergePDF automatically matches template tags with your spreadsheet columns!

### Step 3: Test & Batch Generate
1. Click **"👁️ Generate Row 1 Test"** (100% Free Forever) to verify how the document looks on paper.
2. Enter your output naming pattern, for example: `Certificate_{FullName}.pdf`.
3. Click **"🚀 Generate All PDFs"**.

Within seconds, a clean folder opens with all individual, personalized PDFs ready to print or email!
