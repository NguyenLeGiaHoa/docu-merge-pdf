QUICK START INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Double-click "DocuMergePDF.exe" to launch the application.
2. Drag and drop your Word Template (.docx) into the Template box.
3. Drag and drop your Recipient Roster (.xlsx or .csv) into the Data box.
4. Review the auto-mapped fields and adjust if needed.
5. Set your naming formula (e.g. Certificate_{FullName}_{ID}.pdf).
6. Click "Generate Row 1 Test (Free Forever)" to preview your document.
7. Click "Generate All Individual PDFs (Batch)" to export the entire roster!

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
* 100% Offline & Private: No cloud uploads. Student records and payroll remain confidential on your machine.
* Vector Sharp Rendering: Preserves exact Word formatting, fonts, margins, tables, and colors.
* Dynamic Naming Formula: Custom file names with auto-sanitization of illegal characters.
* Zero Dependencies: Runs standalone on Windows 10 & 11 without requiring Microsoft Word or Office.
